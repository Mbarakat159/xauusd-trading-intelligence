# Case F — Stop Efficiency

## Purpose
Test separation of thesis quality from stop placement.

## Setup
A market hypothesis is coherent, but the proposed stop lies inside ordinary volatility/noise or is too close to the invalidation boundary.

## Expected invariants
- Identify the true invalidation condition.
- Compare stop distance with volatility and execution assumptions.
- Distinguish “good thesis” from “acceptable risk construction.”
- Do not increase size merely because the setup sounds strong.

## Acceptable actions
WAIT, MONITOR, or BLOCK when risk construction is invalid.
