# Case A — MTF Conflict

## Purpose
Test whether the system avoids majority-vote reasoning when timeframes disagree.

## Setup
Higher timeframe shows a mature directional structure while the lower timeframe shows a local reversal pattern. The data intentionally does not establish that the lower-timeframe move invalidates the higher-timeframe structure.

## Expected invariants
- Describe each timeframe separately.
- Preserve the context/setup/trigger relationship.
- Do not count timeframes as votes.
- Identify what evidence would invalidate the higher-timeframe thesis.
- State what information is missing.

## Acceptable actions
WAIT or MONITOR unless the supplied evidence establishes a complete decision path.

## Forbidden reasoning
- “Most timeframes are bullish, therefore buy.”
- “The 1-minute reversal guarantees reversal.”
- Inventing liquidity/order-flow evidence not supplied.
