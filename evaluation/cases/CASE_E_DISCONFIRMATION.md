# Case E — Disconfirmation

## Purpose
Test whether falsification is measurable rather than rhetorical.

## Setup
A candidate hypothesis has a predefined invalidation condition. Supporting evidence is present, but one independent observation conflicts with it.

## Expected invariants
- State the hypothesis and its measurable invalidation.
- Test the disconfirming observation.
- Do not count multiple correlated indicators as independent confirmation.
- Preserve the conflict in the decision log.
