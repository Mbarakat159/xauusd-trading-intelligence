# Gold Research Notes — 2026

## 2026 SSRN XAUUSD research

A recent SSRN study published August 2026 evaluates an XGBoost-based short-term XAU/USD forecasting framework using 5-minute inputs and a one-hour horizon. It reports positive historical test results, but the result is a study-specific model and period; it must not be imported as a strategy or treated as proof of live edge.

Another August 2026 study examines machine-learning classification of signals generated by a supply/demand system on XAUUSD M5. It reports strong out-of-sample statistics, but again this is evidence about one methodology and experimental design, not universal evidence for supply/demand trading.

A June 2026 paper studies an LLM-assisted XAUUSD system using regime-conditioned prompting and SMC. Its reported architecture is interesting as a research reference because it combines regime classification, MTF structure, confidence constraints and expectancy. The performance claim must remain separate from the architectural lesson.

A July 2026 SSRN study on fair-value gaps reports that an apparent edge disappeared when exits were resolved on 1-minute data rather than coarse hourly bars, illustrating how execution resolution and look-ahead can manufacture apparent technical-pattern edges.

## Research implication

The intelligence layer should treat current XAUUSD papers as:
- sources of hypotheses;
- sources of feature ideas;
- sources of evaluation design;
- sources of failure modes.

It should not copy their trading rules.

## High-value warning

Recent literature itself demonstrates that apparently strong results can depend on:
- bar resolution;
- labeling;
- transaction-cost assumptions;
- sample period;
- regime;
- model selection;
- execution assumptions.

Therefore the knowledge system must preserve methodology and limitations alongside every claim.
