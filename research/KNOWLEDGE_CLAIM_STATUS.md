# Knowledge Claim Status

Knowledge objects must distinguish what is believed from what has been tested.

## Claim states
- CLAIMED: stated by a source; not independently verified.
- CORROBORATED: supported by multiple independent credible sources.
- TESTED_ON_OUR_DATA: evaluated under a defined test protocol.
- FORWARD_SUPPORTED: survived a predefined forward/shadow evaluation.
- RESTRICTED: retained for research but prohibited from driving decisions.
- REJECTED: evidence is insufficient, contradicted or methodologically invalid for current use.

A claim can move between states; history must be preserved.

## Required operational definition
Terms that can be interpreted differently must have an explicit operational definition before evaluation. Examples: sweep, breakout, displacement, rejection, trend, range, liquidity pool, order-flow imbalance.

## Evidence is not counted
Ten correlated sources do not equal ten independent confirmations. Evidence quality depends on independence, methodology, data quality, sample design and relevance to the current market/data capability.

## Applicability
Useful When / Weak When are hypotheses about applicability, not guarantees. They must link to observable conditions and remain revisable.

## Decision safety
CLAIMED and CORROBORATED knowledge may inform hypotheses. Only knowledge that passes the applicable promotion gates may become an operational decision dependency.
