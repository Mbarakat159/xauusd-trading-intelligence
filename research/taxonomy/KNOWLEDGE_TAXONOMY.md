# Comprehensive Trading Knowledge Taxonomy

A research taxonomy, not a list of mandatory indicators.

A. Market state: trend, range, transition, compression, expansion, volatility and liquidity regimes.
B. Structure: swings, support/resistance, breakouts, failed breakouts, reversals, continuation, consolidation, gaps.
C. Price action: candles, rejection, displacement, impulse/correction, contextual pattern validity.
D. Liquidity/auction: liquidity pools, sweeps, stop runs, acceptance/rejection, auction theory, value, imbalance.
E. Supply/demand: zones, order blocks, mitigation and institutional-style frameworks, with claims sourced.
F. Volume/flow: volume, VWAP, volume profile, market profile, footprint, DOM, tape/order flow, delta and imbalance.
G. Classical technical analysis: trend, momentum, volatility, oscillators, moving averages, bands, Ichimoku and other established tools.
H. Quantitative methods: momentum, trend following, mean reversion, statistical methods, volatility, correlations, distributions and regime detection.
I. Pattern/phase schools: Wyckoff, Elliott Wave, Fibonacci, harmonics and related frameworks.
J. Macro/event: rates, USD, inflation, labor data, central banks, geopolitical events, commodity relationships and scheduled releases.
K. Multi-timeframe reasoning: context, setup, trigger, execution, invalidation, alignment/conflict and timeframe selection.
L. Trade construction: entry, invalidation, stops, targets, partial exits, trailing, time stops and event handling.
M. Risk: position sizing, volatility-adjusted sizing, fixed fractional risk, exposure, correlation, drawdown, leverage and margin.
N. Execution: spread, slippage, liquidity, latency, order types, fills, broker constraints, sessions and data quality.
O. Evaluation: evidence strength, calibration, robustness, out-of-sample discipline, live-vs-simulated distinction and failure analysis.
