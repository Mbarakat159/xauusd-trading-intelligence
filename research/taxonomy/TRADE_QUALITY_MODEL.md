# Trade Quality Model — Research Specification

Not an executable strategy.

A future intelligence layer should evaluate:
- market regime compatibility
- higher-timeframe context
- setup structure
- entry location
- invalidation clarity
- stop efficiency
- expected reward relative to risk
- liquidity and execution quality
- volatility
- event risk
- conflicting evidence
- thesis fragility
- time-horizon compatibility

A high-quality setup does not automatically justify a larger position. Position size must ultimately be constrained by explicit risk budget, stop distance, exposure and execution conditions. Subjective LLM confidence must never be the sole sizing input.

Future concepts: opportunity quality, invalidation quality, risk efficiency, execution quality, conflict level, recommended action class. Exact schema comes after research and evaluation.
