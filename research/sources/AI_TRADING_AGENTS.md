# AI Trading Agent Research Index

Initial reviewed sources include TauricResearch/TradingAgents, yebof/quant-agent, gentodev/ai-trading-agent, and JesstLe/quant-agent.

## Reusable findings
- Separate analyst roles can reduce single-view reasoning.
- Bullish/bearish research or adversarial review can expose contradictory evidence.
- Hard risk controls should be deterministic where possible.
- Position sizing should use verified market data and explicit risk constraints.
- Persistent decision logs enable later review and calibration.
- Multi-timeframe reasoning should preserve relationships between timeframes.
- Point-in-time integrity and data provenance matter.

## Limitation
These repositories demonstrate architectures and implementations. They do not prove durable trading alpha.
