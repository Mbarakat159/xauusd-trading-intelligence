# Research Plan

## Phase 0 — Scope and provenance
- Define the knowledge taxonomy.
- Establish source quality and provenance rules.
- Preserve original references.
- Separate Fable/agentic reasoning research from trading knowledge.

## Phase 1 — Agentic intelligence
Research publicly available Fable-related material and other AI-agent repositories.
Extract reusable principles for:
- planning
- tool use
- verification
- uncertainty
- memory/state
- evidence handling
- self-review
- failure recovery

Do not copy environment-specific instructions blindly.

## Phase 2 — Trading knowledge universe
Map the full landscape:
- price action
- market structure
- support/resistance
- supply/demand
- Wyckoff
- auction market theory
- liquidity
- SMC/ICT
- volume
- order flow
- market profile
- VWAP
- volatility
- momentum
- trend following
- mean reversion
- breakout/reversal
- quantitative/statistical methods
- Fibonacci/harmonics/Elliott
- classical indicators
- macro/fundamental/news
- sessions and market microstructure
- multi-timeframe analysis

The taxonomy is a research map, not a prescription to use every method.

## Phase 3 — Risk and execution intelligence
Study:
- invalidation quality
- stop placement
- position sizing
- volatility-adjusted risk
- reward/risk
- execution quality
- liquidity/slippage
- event risk
- trade management
- portfolio/exposure constraints

The goal is to understand how setup quality and risk efficiency interact, rather than equating confidence with larger size.

## Phase 4 — Knowledge representation
Convert validated material into structured concepts, relationships, applicability, limitations, evidence and provenance.

## Phase 5 — Evaluation
Build tests that measure whether an AI system can:
- identify relevant analytical families
- distinguish regimes
- compare competing hypotheses
- identify contradictions
- explain invalidation
- select appropriate timeframes/tools
- evaluate trade quality
- reason about risk and execution

## Phase 6 — Integration proposal
Only after the knowledge layer is evaluated should we design an interface to the autonomous agent.
