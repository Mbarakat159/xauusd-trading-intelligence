# Source and Evidence Policy

## Source classes

A. Primary research / original papers / official documentation
B. Reputable research and institutional material
C. Well-documented open-source implementations
D. Practitioner material with explicit methodology
E. Unverified claims, marketing material, or unsupported strategy claims

## Rules

- Preserve the original source URL/repository.
- Record what a source actually claims.
- Do not convert a claim into a fact.
- Record scope: instrument, market, timeframe, period and assumptions when available.
- Record contradictions between sources.
- Prefer multiple independent sources for important concepts.
- Distinguish conceptual knowledge from demonstrated empirical performance.
- A GitHub repository is evidence of an implementation, not proof that its trading method works.
- Backtest results are contextual evidence, not universal truth.
