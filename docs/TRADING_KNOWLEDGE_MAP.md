# Trading Knowledge Map

Initial taxonomy for comprehensive research.

## 1. Market structure
Trend, range, transition, swing structure, BOS/CHoCH terminology, breakouts, reversals, consolidation.

## 2. Price action
Candles, patterns, support/resistance, supply/demand, rejection, displacement, gaps.

## 3. Liquidity and auction
Liquidity concepts, stop runs, sweeps, auction theory, acceptance/rejection, value.

## 4. Volume and order flow
Volume, VWAP, market profile, footprint concepts, DOM, imbalance, execution flow.

## 5. Classical technical analysis
Moving averages, RSI, MACD, stochastic, Williams %R, ADX, ATR, Bollinger Bands, Ichimoku and other established tools.

## 6. Quantitative/statistical methods
Momentum, trend following, mean reversion, volatility, correlations, distributions, statistical inference, regime detection.

## 7. Pattern frameworks
Fibonacci, harmonics, Elliott Wave, Wyckoff and other formal pattern/phase frameworks.

## 8. Macro and event context
Rates, USD, inflation, central-bank events, employment data, geopolitical/event risk, sessions and market hours.

## 9. Multi-timeframe reasoning
Context, setup, trigger, execution and invalidation across multiple time horizons. Avoid assuming one fixed timeframe hierarchy is universally optimal.

## 10. Risk and execution
Position sizing, stop efficiency, invalidation, reward/risk, volatility, spread, slippage, liquidity, exposure, event risk and trade management.

## 11. Evidence quality
Empirical evidence, methodology, sample limitations, live vs simulated results, implementation assumptions and conflicting findings.
